Who should contact us

Our contact channels are open to repeater owners, amateur radio operators, amateur radio clubs, WRA members and other Wisconsin stakeholders.

If your inquiry relates to a specific repeater, including your callsign and site location helps us respond more quickly.

The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance welcomes inquiries from repeater owners, amateur radio operators, clubs, members and other Wisconsin stakeholders. Whether you're asking about a new coordination, an existing system, membership or how to get involved, use the form and we'll respond as soon as we can.
