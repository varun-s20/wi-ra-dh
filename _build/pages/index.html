Who we are

Supporting repeater owners across Wisconsin

The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance, Inc. (WRA) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to the responsible coordination and management of amateur radio repeater systems throughout Wisconsin.

Through sound engineering practices, collaboration and transparency, we help repeater owners, radio clubs and amateur operators make the best possible use of the amateur radio spectrum while minimizing interference and preserving reliable communications.

Whether you are applying for a new repeater, updating an existing system, or searching for coordinated repeater information, WRA provides the resources and guidance the Wisconsin amateur radio community needs.

How coordination works

WRA coordinates Wisconsin's amateur spectrum. ARCS is how the work gets done.

Repeater coordination
  1. 01ApplyRepeater owners submit their system details through ARCS.Applicant
  2. 02Engineering analysisARCS checks the proposal against existing coordinated systems with interference studies and propagation modeling.ARCS
  3. 03Review under published policyCoordinators authorized by WRA review the application under policies set by the Board of Directors.WRA
  4. 04Decision and recordThe decision is documented and kept in Wisconsin's coordination records.WRA

Governance

Built on trust and accountability

WRA is a Wisconsin nonprofit corporation operating as a federally recognized 501(c)(3) charitable organization, governed by a nine-member Board of Directors. Coordination decisions are made under established policies and technical standards rather than individual preference, giving repeater owners confidence that every application receives fair and consistent consideration.

Public service

Supporting public service and emergency communications

Wisconsin's repeater infrastructure plays a critical role during severe weather, public service events and emergency response. Coordinated systems provide dependable communications for ARES, RACES and SKYWARN networks and for community emergency efforts across the state. WRA supports the continued development of reliable repeater networks that strengthen communications capabilities throughout Wisconsin.
