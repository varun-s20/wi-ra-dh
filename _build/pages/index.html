Who we are

Supporting repeater owners across Wisconsin

The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance, Inc. (WRA) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to the responsible coordination and management of amateur radio repeater systems throughout Wisconsin.

Through sound engineering practices, collaboration and transparency, we help repeater owners, radio clubs and amateur operators make the best possible use of the amateur radio spectrum while minimizing interference and preserving reliable communications.

Whether you are applying for a new repeater, updating an existing system, or searching for coordinated repeater information, WRA provides the resources and guidance the Wisconsin amateur radio community needs.
