Become a member

Open to everyone who supports the Alliance's mission

The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance, Inc. is a member-supported nonprofit dedicated to the responsible coordination and stewardship of amateur radio repeater spectrum throughout Wisconsin. Membership is open to everyone who supports the Alliance's mission of promoting reliable communications, sound engineering practices and effective spectrum management.

By becoming a member, you help ensure the long-term sustainability of repeater coordination services and the technology that supports them.

Paying your dues

Secure online payment through Zeffy, and 100% of your $20 goes directly to WRA

  1. 01Pay securely via ZeffyThe Wisconsin Repeater Alliance, Inc. uses Zeffy as its online membership and payment platform. Annual membership dues are $20 per year.
  2. 02No fees to WRAZeffy does not charge platform, transaction or credit card processing fees to nonprofits, so 100% of your payment supports the Alliance.
  3. 03Optional Zeffy contributionZeffy may suggest a contribution to its platform at checkout. It is not part of your dues and is completely voluntary; you can change or remove it.
  4. 04Confirmation by emailZeffy emails a confirmation of your membership transaction. Your membership remains active for one year from the date of payment.

Your membership status and benefits are not affected by whether you choose to make the optional Zeffy contribution. If you have questions about membership payments, renewals or your membership status, please contact the Wisconsin Repeater Alliance.

Membership and governance

Members keep WRA accountable

The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance is governed by a nine-member Board of Directors elected in accordance with the organization's bylaws. Members provide the foundation the Alliance needs to fulfil its mission and remain accountable to the amateur radio community it serves.

As the organization grows, members may have opportunities to take part in committees, volunteer programs, organizational meetings and leadership development.
