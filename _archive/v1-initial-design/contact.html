What is your question about?

Every route reaches WRA at info@wi-ra.org. Choosing a subject helps the right person pick it up.

  • Coordination questions

    Before you apply, about an application in progress, or about an existing coordinated system.

    Ask about coordination

  • Interference reports

    Another repeater is causing interference. Include both systems' details and your site location.

    Report interference

  • Membership

    Joining, renewing, payments through Zeffy or your membership status.

    Ask about membership

  • ARCS and technical questions

    Access to ARCS, accounts, the repeater directory or technical questions about an application.

    Ask about ARCS

  • Board and governance

    Governance, bylaws, policies or matters for the Board of Directors.

    Contact about governance

  • General enquiries and volunteering

    Anything else, including offers of help. See also volunteer roles.

    General enquiry

Wisconsin Repeater Alliance, Inc.Frequency coordination · Wisconsin

WRA coordinates amateur radio repeater frequencies in Wisconsin. ARCS, the Amateur Radio Coordination System, is the platform WRA developed and operates to carry out that work.

501(c)(3) public charity. Recognized by the IRS under section 509(a)(2), effective August 17, 2026. Contributions are tax deductible as allowed by law.
