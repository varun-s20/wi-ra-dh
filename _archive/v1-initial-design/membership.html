01Where dues go

What membership supports

Membership dues directly support the programs, services and technology required to serve Wisconsin's amateur radio community.

  1. Development and maintenance of ARCS, the Amateur Radio Coordination System
  2. Website hosting and digital services
  3. Online repeater coordination tools
  4. Repeater database management
  5. Record preservation and backups
  6. Public repeater directory services
  7. Mapping and engineering resources
  8. Administrative and operational expenses
  9. Educational and outreach initiatives

02Eligibility

Who can join

Membership is open to everyone who supports the Alliance's mission of promoting reliable communications, sound engineering practices and effective spectrum management — repeater owners, clubs, operators and supporters alike.

WRA is governed by a nine-member Board of Directors elected under the bylaws. As the organization grows, members may have opportunities to take part in committees, volunteer programs, organizational meetings and leadership development.

03Member benefits

What members support and receive

  • Fair and transparent spectrum coordination
  • Preservation of Wisconsin repeater records
  • Continued development of the ARCS platform
  • Improved access to repeater information
  • Modern coordination tools and online services
  • Long-term organizational stability and growth

06Questions

Membership questions

What is Zeffy's optional contribution?

During checkout, Zeffy may suggest an optional contribution to support the Zeffy platform. It is not part of your WRA membership dues and is completely voluntary. If you choose not to contribute, WRA still receives 100% of your membership payment. You can accept, change, reduce or remove it — your membership status and benefits are not affected either way.

When does my membership start and end?

Dues are assessed annually from the date you join. Your membership remains active for one year and may be renewed each year thereafter.

Will I get a confirmation?

Yes. Once payment is complete you receive a confirmation email from Zeffy documenting your membership transaction.

Are contributions to WRA tax deductible?

WRA is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) public charity. Donations and contributions are tax deductible to the extent permitted by law. For how this applies to you, consult a tax advisor.

How is WRA funded?

WRA relies on membership dues, coordination-related support, donations, sponsorships and other lawful sources of support. All revenue supports the mission of coordination, spectrum management, record keeping, education and community service.

Prefer to give time?

WRA is a volunteer-driven organization. Technical, coordination, outreach and professional skills are all welcome.
