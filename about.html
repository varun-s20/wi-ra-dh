Who we are

Wisconsin's frequency coordination body

The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance, Inc. is the frequency coordination body responsible for promoting efficient and interference-free operation of amateur radio repeater systems across Wisconsin.

Our purpose is to provide fair, consistent and technically sound frequency coordination while supporting repeater owners, amateur radio clubs, emergency communication organizations and individual operators.

For many years, volunteer coordinators have worked to keep Wisconsin's repeater spectrum organized and accessible. WRA continues that tradition as a modern nonprofit, embracing up-to-date tools and engineering practices that improve coordination, record keeping and spectrum management.

Through cooperation and technical expertise, we help maintain a robust repeater infrastructure that supports everyday communications, public service events, emergency communications and amateur radio experimentation throughout the state.

Mission and vision

Our mission

Engineering-based coordination for every Wisconsin repeater

The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance exists to promote the efficient and responsible use of amateur radio repeater spectrum throughout Wisconsin through engineering-based frequency coordination, technical guidance and collaboration with the amateur radio community. We are committed to minimizing interference, preserving spectrum resources, supporting repeater growth and ensuring reliable communications for current and future generations of amateur radio operators.

Our vision

Wisconsin's trusted resource for repeater coordination

To be Wisconsin's trusted resource for amateur radio repeater coordination by providing fair, transparent and technically sound spectrum management, while fostering cooperation among repeater owners, clubs and amateur operators across the state.

What we do

Coordination, review and records for the whole state

Our goal is to ensure that amateur radio spectrum is used efficiently while providing the greatest possible benefit to the amateur radio community.

WRA and the Amateur Radio Coordination System

One organization, and the platform it built to do the work

The organization

Wisconsin Repeater Alliance (WRA)

WRA is responsible for amateur radio repeater frequency coordination, spectrum management, policies, governance and oversight. Coordination decisions remain under the authority of the organization and its governing structure.

How WRA is governed

The platform

Amateur Radio Coordination System (ARCS)

ARCS is the technology system developed and operated by WRA to perform the engineering and coordination functions: propagation modeling, interference studies, database management, application processing and coordination workflows.

Governance

How WRA is governed

WRA is a Wisconsin nonprofit corporation operating as a federally recognized 501(c)(3) charitable organization.

The organization is governed by a nine-member Board of Directors elected in accordance with its bylaws. This structure means coordination decisions are made under established policies and Board oversight, not by any single individual, and it builds accountability and transparency into everything we do. Our bylaws are published so the community can see exactly how the organization is run.
