Welcome to the new Wisconsin Repeater Alliance website

Wisconsin Repeater Alliance1 min read

The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance is pleased to launch its new website. Here you can learn about frequency coordination, apply for repeater coordination, become a member, volunteer, and stay up to date with announcements from the Alliance. As a Wisconsin nonprofit governed by a nine-member Board of Directors, WRA is committed to fair, transparent and technically sound coordination for Wisconsin’s amateur radio community. Explore the site and get in touch with any questions.
