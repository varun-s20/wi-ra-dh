<h2> Become a member
<text> The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance, Inc. is a member-supported nonprofit dedicated to the responsible coordination and stewardship of amateur radio repeater spectrum throughout Wisconsin. Membership is open to everyone who supports the Alliance’s mission of promoting reliable communications, sound engineering practices and effective spectrum management. By becoming a member, you help ensure the long-term sustainability of repeater coordination services and the technology that supports them.
<h2> Annual membership dues
<text> Dues are assessed annually from the date you join. Your membership remains active for one year and may be renewed each year thereafter.

<h2> Where your membership dollars go
<li> Development and maintenance of the Amateur Radio Coordination System (ARCS)
<li> Website hosting and digital services
<li> Online repeater coordination tools
<li> Repeater database management
<li> Record preservation and backups

<li> Mapping and engineering resources
<li> Administrative and operational expenses

<text> Every membership helps strengthen the technical infrastructure and operational capability needed to provide modern, reliable repeater coordination for Wisconsin.
<h2> Member benefits
<text> As a member of the Wisconsin Repeater Alliance, you help support:
<p> Fair and transparent spectrum coordination
<p> Preservation of Wisconsin repeater records
<p> Continued development of the ARCS platform
<p> Improved access to repeater information
<p> Modern coordination tools and online services
<p> Long-term organizational stability and growth
<text> Most importantly, your membership demonstrates your commitment to preserving and strengthening amateur radio communications throughout Wisconsin.
<h2> Membership and governance
<text> The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance is governed by a nine-member Board of Directors elected in accordance with the organization’s bylaws. Members provide the foundation the Alliance needs to fulfill its mission and remain accountable to the amateur radio community it serves. As the organization grows, members may have opportunities to take part in committees, volunteer programs, organizational meetings and leadership development.
