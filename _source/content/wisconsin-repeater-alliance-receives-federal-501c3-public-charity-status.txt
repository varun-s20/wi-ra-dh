<h5> September 24, 2026
<p> The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance is proud to announce that we have officially been recognized by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charity.
<p> With an effective date of August 17, 2026, this determination represents a major milestone in the growth and long-term sustainability of our organization. The IRS has classified the Wisconsin Repeater Alliance as a public charity under Section 509(a)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code, further validating our commitment to serving the amateur radio and public service communications community through educational, technical, and charitable activities.
<h5> What This Means
<li> Donations and contributions to the Wisconsin Repeater Alliance are now tax deductible to the extent permitted by law.
<li> The organization is eligible to receive tax-deductible gifts, bequests, and charitable contributions.
<li> Our federal tax-exempt status formally recognizes the public benefit mission of supporting and improving amateur radio communications infrastructure throughout Wisconsin.
<p> This achievement is the result of significant effort dedicated to establishing a strong organizational foundation, including governance documents, policies, financial controls, strategic planning, and organizational oversight. We extend our sincere appreciation to everyone who contributed their time, expertise, and support throughout this process.
<h5> Looking Ahead
<p> Receiving federal recognition as a charitable organization is an important step, but it is only the beginning. The Wisconsin Repeater Alliance remains focused on its mission to promote and support reliable amateur radio repeater systems, encourage technical advancement, foster cooperation among system owners and operators, and preserve communications resources for public service, emergency communications, education, and amateur radio activities across Wisconsin.
<p> As we move forward, we look forward to expanding programs, partnerships, and initiatives that strengthen Wisconsin’s amateur radio community while providing lasting value to operators, repeater owners, and the public we serve.
<h5> Thank You
<p> We would like to thank our volunteers, supporters, and partners who have helped make this possable.
