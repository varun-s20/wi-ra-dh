<h2> Understanding Zeffy's optional contribution
<text> During checkout, Zeffy may suggest an optional contribution to help support the Zeffy platform. This contribution is not part of your Wisconsin Repeater Alliance membership dues and is completely voluntary. If you choose not to contribute to Zeffy, the Wisconsin Repeater Alliance still receives 100% of your membership payment. You are free to accept the suggested contribution, change the amount, reduce it, or remove it entirely. Your membership status and benefits are not affected either way.
